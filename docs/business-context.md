# Business Context

## Company

**Musterhandel Retail GmbH** is a fictional online retailer selling electronics and household products.

## Business situation

The customer support team receives a high volume of tickets. Employees spend time understanding the customer's issue and writing consistent responses.

The company wants a prototype that helps employees prepare response drafts faster while keeping humans responsible for the final response.

## Primary user

A customer support employee who needs to:
- Understand a ticket quickly.
- Receive a useful first draft.
- Identify missing information.
- Avoid unsupported promises.
- Communicate clearly and professionally.

## Supported ticket categories

1. Order status
2. Returns and exchanges
3. Damaged products
4. Billing and refunds
5. Order changes and complaints

## Prototype boundaries

The application does not need:
- A real order database.
- Real customer data.
- Email sending.
- Payment integration.
- Automatic refunds.
- A production authentication system.
